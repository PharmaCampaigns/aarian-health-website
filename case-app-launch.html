Launching an app to HCPs, and getting real adoption.

A leading healthcare company set out to launch an app for healthcare professionals, built to help them better support their patients and save them time. The risk with any launch like this is familiar: plenty of downloads, very little use. The brief was adoption, not vanity.

Client Leading healthcare company Audience Healthcare professionals Goal Real, sustained adoption

The challenge

A great app is not a used app.

HCPs are time-poor and sceptical of anything that adds a step to their day. A launch email and an app-store listing were never going to be enough. The company needed HCPs to understand the value quickly, fit the app into real workflows, and keep coming back, not just install it and forget it.

Our approach

Design the experience, not just the launch.

We built the launch as an experience program: a guided path that introduced the app in the context of the HCP's own work, showed the time saving early, and supported them through the first uses, the moments where most launches quietly lose people.

What we delivered

From download to daily habit.

Design

A value-first onboarding

We framed the app around the HCP's problem and showed the time saving in the first session, not the fifth.

Deliver

Guided experience program

A structured path walked HCPs through the moments that matter, with human support where it counted.

Support

Through the first uses

We stayed with users past install, through the early sessions where adoption is won or lost.

Prove

Engagement, not just installs

We tracked real usage so the client could see adoption building, and act on what was working.

The outcome

Adoption that stuck.

By treating the launch as an experience rather than an announcement, the company moved HCPs past the install and into genuine, repeat use, the outcome that actually helps patients and justifies the investment.
