ActivationPatient supportSafety

Every patient trained to use the device safely.

A medical device manufacturer needed to make sure every patient understood how to handle its device correctly. The brief was simple to say and hard to do: communicate the key aspects of correct, safe use, consistently, to every patient at the pharmacy.

Client Medical device manufacturer Goal Safe, consistent onboarding Setting Community pharmacy

The challenge

Consistency is a safety issue.

When a device is placed but used incorrectly, outcomes suffer and so does trust in the product. The manufacturer could not rely on the pack insert alone: they needed a human, repeatable way to make sure the key handling messages actually reached every patient, the same way, every time.

Our approach

Put the pharmacist at the centre.

We designed a pharmacist-led patient support program that used the counter as the training moment. We structured the conversation around the critical safety points, gave pharmacies the tools to deliver it consistently, and captured that it had been done, so activation and compliance moved together.

What we delivered

A trained patient, every time.

Design

The critical-few messages

We distilled correct handling into the points that matter most for safety, in language a busy counter could deliver.

Enable

Pharmacies equipped to train

Structured prompts and support helped pharmacists deliver the same, correct guidance to every patient.

Deliver

Coordinator-supported reach

Our team drove participation so no pharmacy was left doing it alone, and coverage held up across the network.

Prove

Documented completion

Interventions were recorded, giving the manufacturer evidence that safety training was actually happening.

The outcome

Safer use, provable delivery.

The manufacturer moved from hoping patients read the instructions to knowing a pharmacist had walked them through safe use, with a documented record to stand behind.
