Accelerating customer acquisition in community pharmacy.

A large healthcare multinational needed to speed new customer acquisition in the community pharmacy channel and rapidly build market share, while making the job of its pharmacy sales reps easier, not harder.

Client Healthcare multinational Channel Community pharmacy Focus New customer acquisition

The challenge

Growth that depended on rep reach.

Acquisition was capped by how many stores a field team could physically reach, and how much of each visit was spent on administration rather than selling. The client wanted to build share quickly, but the reps were stretched, coverage was uneven, and there was little visibility of what actually happened once a rep left the store.

Our approach

Design the play, then run it.

We designed an acquisition program delivered through our own technology and coordinator team, so that reach no longer depended on physical visits alone. We took repetitive tasks off the reps, structured the pharmacy conversation, and connected every interaction to a single view the client's national account managers could see in real time.

What we delivered

An acquisition engine, store by store.

Design

Targeted store list & conversation

We prioritised the pharmacies most likely to move the number, and built a simple, compliant conversation the channel could run consistently.

Deliver

Coordinator-led reach beyond the van run

Campaign coordinators reached the stores reps could not, so coverage stopped being a function of drive time.

Enable

Reps freed to sell

Administrative steps were absorbed by the platform, giving the field team more time in front of the customer.

Prove

A live view of acquisition

Every interaction fed a dashboard so leadership could see participation and momentum as it built.

The outcome

Wider reach, faster share.

The program extended acquisition beyond the limits of the field team, gave reps back selling time, and gave the client a clear, defensible view of what the pharmacy channel was contributing to the funnel, campaign by campaign.
