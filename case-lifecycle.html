Turning a pharmacy sale into a lasting relationship.

A large healthcare multinational wanted to enhance customer retention through email and SMS marketing, but had no way to capture patient details at the moment the product was purchased in the pharmacy. Without the data, the lifecycle program had nowhere to start.

Client Healthcare multinational Goal Retention & lifecycle Focus Compliant data capture

The challenge

A blind spot at the point of purchase.

The purchase happened in the pharmacy, off the brand's platforms, so the customer relationship ended at the counter. There was appetite for a proper lifecycle program, but no compliant, consistent way to capture consent and contact details where the sale actually took place.

Our approach

Capture the moment, respect the rules.

We designed a data-capture flow inside our own technology that fit the pharmacy day and the compliance requirements, so patient details and consent could be collected cleanly at the point of purchase, then fed straight into an email and SMS lifecycle program.

What we delivered

From counter to lifecycle.

Consent-first capture

A simple, compliant way to collect patient details and consent at the pharmacy, built to respect quality use of medicines and privacy.

Clean data into the program

Captured records flowed into the client's email and SMS journeys, with no re-keying and a clear audit trail.

Lifecycle that runs itself

Onboarding, adherence and re-engagement messages triggered automatically, turning a one-off purchase into an ongoing relationship.

The outcome

A retention channel that didn't exist before.
