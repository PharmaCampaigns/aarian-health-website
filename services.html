What we do

We design the strategy, and own the delivery.

Aarian is a healthcare delivery consultancy. We work across the whole funnel, acquisition, activation and retention, and across the whole ecosystem, from brands and professional bodies to pharmacies, clinicians and patients. The through-line is simple: we plan it, then we run it, and we prove it happened.

Start a conversation → See the work

The funnel

Three stages, each with its own job.

We work across all three, and the through-line between them is where growth compounds.

Acquisition

Looking to win new patients or customers?

We help teams acquire new patients and customers, whether it is a launch that needs traction, a product that needs speed, or a legacy brand labelled a cash cow. Our strength is showing the impact the pharmacy channel can have on your acquisition funnel.

Activation

Got attention but not action?

Devices are placed but usage is low, consumables sales lag, or repeat purchases do not meet expectations. We weave technology into your plans to unlock the X factor and turn attention into action, while keeping your medical and compliance managers comfortable.

Retention

How do you keep them coming back?

We design bespoke digital solutions that help companies and healthcare providers build long-term engagement, where product portfolio transitions and loyalty matter most. The hardest to achieve, and the most rewarding when it is done right.

The engagement

Empowering healthcare with tailored services.

Five capabilities we bring to every program, mixed to fit the goal in front of us.

🛠️

Tech-enabled solutions

We build technology around real brand challenges. From WhatsApp integrations to workflow design, we create solutions that improve efficiency and deliver impact, not technology for its own sake.

🏪

Pharmacy channel engagement

We help brands engage with pharmacists, pharmacy assistants, banner groups and individual stores. Our programs drive uptake and advocacy where it matters most: at the counter.

🤝

Patient support programs

We design systems and human-led services that support patients: telehealth consults, peer support, adherence tools and quality use of medicines programs. Our diabetes expertise, including access to Credentialled Diabetes Educators, adds proven value for patients and brands alike.

📈

HCP & pharmacy insights

We capture feedback and insights from healthcare professionals and pharmacy teams. This evidence helps brand and medical teams make confident decisions, and stand behind them.

🎓

Learning & education

We deliver training for pharmacists, pharmacy assistants and healthcare professionals. Our diabetes programs, led by Credentialled Diabetes Educators, build clinical confidence and improve patient outcomes.

🧠

AI & automation

We use generative AI to strengthen acquisition, activation and retention, from pharmacy targeting to engagement tracking, making campaigns more precise, adaptive and effective.

How an engagement runs

From first conversation to proof at the review.

A clear path from goal to delivered, documented outcome.

Understand the goal

We start with the number you carry and the barrier in the way, then map where pharmacy, patients and HCPs fit the result.

Design the program

We shape the acquisition, activation or retention play, with compliance, medical and commercial realities built in from the start.

Deliver it

We run it through our own technology and coordinator team, store by store, conversation by conversation, so it actually happens.

Prove it

Dashboards show interventions delivered, patients reached and ROI, so you can prove value at every review meeting.

Scale what works

We pilot to prove real-world impact, then scale, always aligned with commercial realities and evidence.

Stay on

We don't hand you a deck and leave. We stay beside you through portfolio transitions and ongoing growth.

Tell us what you're trying to move.

Give us the goal, and we'll show you how we would design and deliver it, across the pharmacies, clinicians and patients that matter.
